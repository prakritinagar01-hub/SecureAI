      Think before you click

      Three quick rules that stop most phishing attacks.

      Never share your OTP

      Banks and apps never ask for OTPs, PINs or passwords by call, SMS or chat.

      Pause on urgency

      "Act now" and "account will be blocked" are pressure tactics. Slow down and verify.

      Check the link

      Open the official app or type the website yourself instead of tapping a link.
